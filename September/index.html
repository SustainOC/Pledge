Before you pick a day

Kelowna campus is served by routes 1, 4, 8 and 12. Penticton by 2, 4 and 15. Vernon by 6 and 90. Salmon Arm has no campus route listed, so cycling or carpooling is the practical option there.

Parking Services can sort you out with secure bicycle parking at parking@okanagan.bc.ca, and OC Commutes matches you with people driving your way. Scan a code, or tap it if you are already on your phone.
